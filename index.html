Official Privacy Policy

Strictly Local Network Architecture • Zero Data Collection • 100% GDPR & COPPA Compliant

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy

Universal TV Remote Control is built on a Local-First Architecture. We believe your entertainment habits are personal. Unlike cloud-dependent remotes, our application connects directly from your smartphone to your Smart TV over your private local area network (LAN) or built-in infrared emitter (IR).

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No Browsing or Viewing History: We never log what channels you watch, what streaming shows you launch, or what you browse.
  • No Keystroke Logging: All inputs (volume, power, navigation) remain strictly within your local network.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or physical address.

3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

  • Wi-Fi & Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Required solely to discover Smart TVs on your local subnet (SSDP, mDNS, UPnP).
  • Infrared (TRANSMIT_IR): Required to send infrared signals to non-smart televisions if your phone hardware supports it.
  • Vibrate: Used to provide subtle haptic tactile feedback when pressing remote buttons.
  • In-App Billing (BILLING): Managed securely through Google Play Billing for VIP / PRO subscription features.

4. Advertising & In-App Purchases

Free versions of the app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob adheres to Google Play developer policies and industry privacy standards. Upgrading to VIP / PRO permanently disables all advertising across the application.
